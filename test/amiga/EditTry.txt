EditTry -- WaveSmith by hand, through the card's player

A copy of the test single is opened from RAM:WaveSmith: 40 s, a lead-in
with the needle's thump and clicks, three chords, a fade, the run-out
groove's click every 1.8 s. Nothing outside RAM:WaveSmith is written.

Try:
 1  Listen (beside In): 3 s from In through the card, the orange play
    head running. Listen beside Out: the last 3 s before Out.
 2  Play, then Space: it pauses ("Paused at ...") and the head stays;
    Space or Play again goes on from there. Stop sends the marker back
    to the start; |< and >| put it at the start or the end.
 3  Zoom in (At In) and Play: the head should glide, and the view turn
    a page when the head runs off it -- the next page already drawn.
 4  Click on the sound while it plays: the card jumps there. Stopped,
    a click puts the marker there and Play starts from it.
 5  The mouse wheel over the waveform zooms where the pointer is (away
    from you: in); Shift and the wheel scroll; over the overview strip
    at the top the view goes there.
 6  Drag over the sound to mark a part; View/Zoom to selection fills
    the view with it.
 7  Rest the pointer on a handle, on the sound, on a button: bubbles
    (none over the waveform before a file is open).
 8  Drag In or Out -- by its line or its little flag in the waveform,
    by its line in the overview strip at the top: the title gets a *
    (changed, not saved). Quit or
    the close gadget asks -- in a window centred on WaveSmith's: Save,
    Discard or Cancel (Return the first, Esc the last).
 9  Drop a sound file's icon on the window: it opens (asking first if
    the open one has changes).
10  The Edit menu: drag over a part, Cut out (Right-Amiga X) asks --
    Join closes the gap (a thin mark where it was), Silence leaves a
    flat tinted stretch, the length as it was. Right-Amiga Z undoes it,
    Right-Amiga Y does it again; every handle move is a step too, and
    Undo back to the file as opened takes the * away.
11  Mark a part, Right-Amiga Shift Z: the view zooms to it, as View/Zoom
    to selection does (Right-Amiga Z alone is Undo).
12  A click puts the play marker; Project/Split at the cursor writes
    Trim-1.wav (In to the marker) and Trim-2.wav (the marker to Out)
    beside the file, the cuts in them -- the file and the window stay
    as they were.
13  Save (Right-Amiga S): the gauge counts the per cent and says the
    time left; Cancel (or Esc) calls the save off, the file as it was.
    Saved -- the cuts in it -- the file is opened again (Cancel calls
    that reading off), and Undo starts anew.
14  The dotted bar under the overview strip at the top: drag it down,
    the overview grows and the waveform shrinks; drag it back. Resize
    the window: the two keep their shares.
15  Settings: Snap to zero crossings? off -- a handle let go stays where
    it was let go (the status line no longer says "snapped"); on again.
    Lead and tail...: slide or type the milliseconds, the seconds beside
    them follow; Use moves In and Out to the new suggestion while
    nothing was changed yet (no * in the title), Esc or Cancel leaves
    everything as it was. Keep a backup? on, then Save: the old file
    stays as Trim.wav.bak (its level map as Trim.prl.bak). Create icons?
    (on): every new file gets the system's sound icon. Save settings:
    ENVARC:WaveSmith.prefs -- the bar's place in it -- read at the next
    start.
16  Project/Save as... (Right-Amiga A): name a new file; it is written
    (its icon and map with it), and the window works on it from then on
    -- the original as it was. Its own name saves in place; a name that
    is there is asked about first. For Single.flac or Single.m4a, Save
    asks this way too, <name>-trim.wav filled in.
17  The Play menu: Play/Pause (Space), Play selection (Shift Space: the
    part marked, and only it), Stop, Listen at In, Listen at Out; Loop?
    on: what was asked for plays again and again until Stop.
18  The cursor keys: a click on the sound, then Left and Right move the
    play marker a column of the view each (Shift a page, Ctrl to the
    start or the end); drag or nudge In (or Out) and the keys move that
    handle instead -- held down, one Right-Amiga Z takes the whole run
    back. Up and Down zoom in and out where it is.
19  Markers: Edit/Find tracks puts one at each track's start (a long
    side is measured first: the gauge, Cancel); Return (or Enter) puts
    one at the play marker -- tap it while it plays; click a marker and
    Del takes it away, drag one to move it; Undo takes each change back.
    Project/Split at markers writes <name>-01.wav, -02 ... beside the
    file. Save writes <name>.cue beside it, and the markers come back
    from it when the file is opened again.
20  Settings / Play through: Automatic (the card, else AHI), The Prisma
    card, or AHI; Settings / AHI mode... picks AHI's mode. Without the
    card (the A1200, the A2000), Play and Listen play through AHI.
21  Rest the pointer on the sound, on Save, on Play, and press Help:
    the manual opens at the page about it, in the system's language;
    Project/Help... opens its first page.
22  Close the window (or Right-Amiga Q): the manual's window goes too.

On the A4000T, Single.flac and Single.m4a open too (decoded first; the
gauge, the time left and Cancel there as well -- WARNING: Cancel for
Single.m4a needs prisma.library 3.2 and prismadsp.library 1.2; with
older ones a chip decode called off leaves the card unable to decode
until it is reset).

Then the saved file is copied to Edit/Trim.wav beside this script, for
the Mac's check (tools/make_edit_single.py --check).

(Space pages on, Q leaves this text.)
